### 🎙️ The Brief

I enjoy building software from first principles—bridging the gap between raw data, machine learning, and clean, accessible user workflows. Currently pursuing my degree in **Information Technology at TCET, Mumbai**, contributing to open source, and active across tech hackathons & developer events.

* 🌾 **Social Impact:** `Crop Insurance Bridge` — Modernizing agricultural workflows to digitize insurance claim verifications for rural communities.
* 🤖 **AI / ML Engineering:** `Supply Chain Risk Predictor` — Machine learning engine utilizing Random Forest algorithms to model freight delays and supply disruptions.
* 🛠️ **Creator Platform:** `XYZ Platform` — Scalable media and community interface designed for digital creators to distribute assets and engage audiences.
* 👥 **Community & Open Source:** Official contributor at **Open Source Connect India (2026)** and Community Lead for **PYQ Website Updates** (the largest WhatsApp engineering community at TCET).
